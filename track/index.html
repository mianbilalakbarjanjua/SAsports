Track your order

Know where your parcel is, in three simple steps.

1

When you order, your WhatsApp message carries an order number like SA-123456.

2

When your parcel is dispatched, we send you the courier name and the courier tracking number.

3

Use that number on the courier’s website, or ask us for the latest status below.
